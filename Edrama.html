A Brief introduction

Dramas that are in English are commonly referred to as "English dramas" or "English-language dramas." These terms indicate that the primary language used in the drama is English. English-language dramas can originate from English-speaking countries such as the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, and others. They may also be produced in non-English-speaking countries for international audiences or as part of co-productions involving multiple countries. The term "English dramas" is used to distinguish these productions from dramas in other languages.
