A Brief introduction

Korean dramas, 한국 드라마, are television series in the Korean language that are made in South Korea.
They are popular worldwide, especially in Asia, partially due to the spread of Korean popular culture (the "Korean Wave") and their widespread availability via streaming services, which often offer subtitles in multiple languages.
